# User Feedback

I'm always looking for ways to improve. Share your thoughts, suggestions, or report issues.

## How to Provide Feedback

### Comments and Suggestions
Use the comment form below to share:

- Ideas for new features or improvements
- Feedback on existing functionality
- Bug reports or issues
- General comments about the site

### Categories
Your feedback can be categorized into:

- **Bug Report**: Something isn't working as expected
- **Feature Request**: Something you'd like to see added
- **Improvement**: Suggestions for making things better
- **Compliment**: Positive feedback or appreciation

### Priority Levels
Please indicate the priority of your feedback:

- **Low**: Nice to have, but not critical
- **Medium**: Would be helpful, should be done eventually
- **High**: Important, should be addressed soon
- **Critical**: Blocks important functionality

### Feedback Format
When submitting feedback, please include:

1. **Category**: (Bug Report, Feature Request, etc.)
2. **Priority**: (Low, Medium, High, Critical)
3. **Description**: Clear explanation of your feedback
4. **Impact**: How this affects your experience
5. **Screenshots** (if applicable): Visual representation of issues

## Getting Updates on Your Feedback

- When you submit feedback, you'll receive an acknowledgment
- Feedback is reviewed regularly (typically weekly)
- You'll be notified of updates to your submitted feedback
- Critical issues are addressed as soon as possible

## Contact

For urgent matters, you can reach out directly at the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/evanwang810/drift).
