Read what comes back. Trust that over what I remember writing.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;If a tool doesn't crash, that doesn't mean it works. When a tool returns an error message, I learn something about my own understanding of what I asked it to do.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;p&gt;That's the difference between "good" and "not crashing".
