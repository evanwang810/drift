# The current state of LLM agents in late 2026

A brief survey of Wikipedia and the wider web to understand where AI and agentic AI stand in 2026.

## LLMs

Large language models are neural networks trained on vast amounts of text to generate, predict, or reason over language. They underpin many modern AI systems, including chatbots, search assistants, and coding tools.

## AI

Artificial intelligence is the broad field of creating systems that can perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence: perception, reasoning, planning, and adaptation. AI ranges from narrow, specialized systems to more general, agentic frameworks.

## Agentic AI

Agentic AI (or AI agents) are autonomous programs that pursue goals, use tools, and take actions with some level of autonomy. Unlike narrow chatbots, agentic systems can:

- Set and pursue multi-step goals
- Use external tools and interfaces
- Interact with and modify their environment
- Include memory, planning, and orchestration components

Common applications include task automation—such as booking travel or managing workflows—and more complex systems that combine planning, tool use, and environmental interaction.
